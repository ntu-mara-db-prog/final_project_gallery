# Screenshots

One image per group, named by group number:

```
group-01.png
group-02.png
...
group-14.png
```

- `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg` or `.webp` all work.
- Aim for a 16:9 screenshot of your best screen, about 1600×900.
- Images of other shapes are cropped to 16:9 automatically, keeping the top of the screen.
- A group without an image shows a placeholder.
